ATILIANDOZZY GLOBAL NIG LTD is a professional metal fabrication and construction company specializing in high quality wrought iron and steel works. We design, fabricate and install custom wrought iron gates, staircase rails, balustrades, carports, tank stands, steel structures and frameless glass balustrades.

We serve residential, commercial and industrial clients, providing strong, durable and modern solutions tailored to each client's project. Our goal is to deliver quality workmanship, beautiful designs, reliable installation and excellent customer service from consultation and quotation through to project completion.
